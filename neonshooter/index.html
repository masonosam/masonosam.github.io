Neon Shooter

// Element Control //

Smash through neon gates in a fast-paced endless arcade shooter where elemental strategy meets cyberpunk style. Choose fire, water, or wood and blast your way to the top of the scoreboard!

// Game Mechanics //

Element Cycle

Fire beats Wood, Wood beats Water, Water beats Fire. Use the counter element for triple damage. Switch on the fly and master the rock-paper-scissors strategy.

Power Up Your Ship

Destroy gates to collect stat-boosting rewards. ATK for more damage, SPD for faster fire rate, and AMMO to launch multiple bullets at once.

Score Combos

Reach milestones for bonus points. Destroy both gates in a pair for a x3 multiplier. The deeper you go, the higher the score climbs.

One-Touch Controls

Drag to move, hold to fire, tap to switch elements. Simple to learn, impossible to master. Play anywhere with intuitive touch controls.

Adaptive Difficulty

The game gets faster and tougher as your score grows. Adaptive difficulty ensures every run is a challenge. Plus, revive for a second chance at a new high score.
